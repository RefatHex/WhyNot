🔒 How your privacy is protected
  • Nothing you type is saved anywhere. The link itself carries everything.
  • It all sits after the # in the link, a part browsers never send to the website's server.
  • It's scrambled so names aren't readable in the link, but it isn't encrypted: someone with the link could decode it.
  • The page doesn't track anything. Their answer never leaves their phone unless they send it to you.
